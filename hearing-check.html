Your hearing is assessed by our audiology team during your scheduled appointment.

At your appointment: An audiologist will discuss your hearing concerns and recommend suitable assessments. A professional evaluation is needed to determine your hearing levels and next steps.
Hearing changed suddenly?If your hearing has suddenly become worse, seek urgent medical care rather than waiting for a routine appointment.
